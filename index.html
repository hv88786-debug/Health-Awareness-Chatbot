Quick Guidance Cards

Fever Care Steps
  • Rest and monitor temperature regularly
  • Stay hydrated with water and fluids
  • Use light clothing and ventilation
  • Seek care if fever exceeds 102°F
Cold Care Steps
  • Keep warm and avoid cold exposure
  • Warm fluids and steam inhalation help
  • Rest adequately to support recovery
  • Watch for worsening cough or breathing
Emergency Warning Signs
  • Difficulty breathing or chest pain
  • Persistent high fever above 103°F
  • Confusion, fainting, or severe weakness
  • If present, seek emergency care now
